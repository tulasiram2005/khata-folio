# Design decision

Selected the shop passbook. A forest-green masthead (#17352B), warm paper (#F6F1E5), dark ink (#202B32) and oxide-red exceptions (#98351E) recall a handwritten running account. Serif names and headings sit beside aligned monospace amounts. A narrow facing page holds proposals while the ledger stays visible.

Considered a reconciliation desk with charcoal (#252C30), white (#FAFAF8), amber (#ECB653), condensed headings and a split comparison view. It prioritizes import over everyday entry. A receipt roll with ivory (#FFF9E9), navy (#172540), monospaced labels and a single chronological strip works on a phone but hides the account index.
